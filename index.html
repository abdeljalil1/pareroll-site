Pareroll

Pare down your camera roll. Every photo is analysed on your iPhone and never uploaded.

Getting started

Open Pareroll and tap Allow Access to Photos. You can share your whole library or only some photos. The scan starts by itself and runs on your iPhone; the home screen shows how much space you could free in each category.

What Pareroll finds

Duplicates: the same picture saved more than once, even resized or re-saved months later. Similar shots: bursts and retakes that look alike and were taken moments apart. Screenshots, blurry photos and large videos (over 50 MB by default; change the size in Settings).

Reviewing and deleting

In Duplicates and Similar shots, Pareroll marks the best photo of each group to keep and the rest to delete; tap any photo to change that, or Keep all. In the other categories, swipe left to mark a photo for deletion and right to keep it, or switch to the grid. Your choices are saved as you go.

Nothing is deleted until you tap Delete and confirm in the iOS prompt. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can recover them for 30 days.

Pareroll Pro

Free includes scanning, every category, both review modes, one free clean-up of up to 1 GB, and then up to 30 deletions a day. Pareroll Pro removes the daily limit, keeps the best of every group in one tap, and lets you choose how strict "similar" is. Pro is a one-time lifetime purchase with no trial and nothing that renews, or a yearly subscription with a 3-day free trial. There is no weekly plan.

To cancel the yearly plan, open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone. If you reinstall, tap Restore Purchases in Pareroll's Settings.

Common questions

Some photos don't appear.
Pareroll only sees the photos you shared and never downloads from iCloud. If you chose "Limit Access", tap Choose more photos on the home screen. Photos stored only in iCloud are skipped.

The space didn't come back after deleting.
iOS frees the space once Recently Deleted is emptied, or after 30 days.

Two different photos were grouped as similar.
Pro users can choose Strict in Settings. Similar photos must also have been taken within minutes of each other.
