Privacy Policy

Pareroll for iPhone · Last updated November 11, 2026

What Pareroll collects

Nothing. Pareroll does not collect, upload, sell, or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising code, no third-party code, and no tracking. The app contains no networking code of its own.

Your photos and videos

Pareroll reads only the photos and videos you allow it to access. It analyses them on your iPhone with Apple's frameworks (Photos, Vision and Core Graphics) to find duplicates, similar shots, screenshots, blurry photos and large videos. Nothing is sent to Pareroll, to Apple, or to anyone else. Photos stored only in iCloud are skipped, never downloaded.

The scan results, your keep/delete choices, your settings and your space-saved totals are stored only on your iPhone, inside Pareroll's private storage. Deleting the app removes them.

Deleting photos

Pareroll deletes a photo or video only after you confirm in the iOS prompt. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days.

Purchases

Pareroll Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; Pareroll only learns whether Pro is active, never your name or payment details.

Children

Pareroll does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
